If you are learning networks, send real packets

Make the trade-offs visible

  • Build a tiny app that sends data over TCP and UDP.
  • See reliability, ordering, retries, and speed as behavior you can observe.

How to build your beginner’s advantage now

Practice like this

  1. Start from first principles before you reach for prompts.
  2. Build small, personal, useful systems while you study.
  3. Use AI as a thought partner while you keep reasoning active.
  4. Write tests, explain trade-offs, and keep evidence.
  5. Ask better questions than your peers.

Own your judgment

When you can explain it, you can improve it.

Respect boundaries

Know what data you are sharing and why.

Prefer useful constraints

Good conventions and strong frameworks help both humans and AI stay honest.
