2026 is the best time to be a maker

For the first time, students can actually live the lessons while they are still learning them.

2008 reality

  • Ambitious ideas required more setup time.
  • Curiosity often had to wait for tooling.
  • Many concepts were harder to experience directly.

2026 reality

  • AI lowers the cost of experimentation.
  • You can build while learning theory.
  • Making has become a practical way to study.
This is one of the best times in history to become a maker because curiosity can turn into working systems faster than ever.

If you are learning operating systems, build a tiny world

Live the lesson

  • Vibe-code a tiny hello-world flavor of Linux from scratch.
  • Let boot, memory, processes, and interrupts become felt experiences.
  • Use AI to accelerate the scaffolding while you stay close to the meaning.

Boot

How does anything start?

Memory

Process

In 2008, this was intellectually exciting and time-intensive. In 2026, it is practical enough to try while you learn.

If you are learning networks, send real packets

Make the trade-offs visible

  • Build a tiny app that sends data over TCP and UDP.
  • See reliability, ordering, retries, and speed as behavior you can observe.
  • Watch the concept become real as the system responds in front of you.

How to build your beginner’s advantage now

Practice like this

  1. Start from first principles before you reach for prompts.
  2. Build small, personal, useful systems while you study.
  3. Use AI as a thought partner while you keep reasoning active.
  4. Write tests, explain trade-offs, and keep evidence.
  5. Ask better questions than your peers.

Own your judgment

When you can explain it, you can improve it.

Respect boundaries

Know what data you are sharing and why.

Prefer useful constraints

Good conventions and strong frameworks help both humans and AI stay honest.
